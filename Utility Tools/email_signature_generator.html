integration_instructions How to Add Your Signature to Gmail

1
Click the blue "Copy for Gmail" button below to copy the formatted signature directly to your clipboard.
2
Open Gmail, click the Settings Gear ⚙️ in the top-right, and select "See all settings".
3
In the General tab, scroll down to the Signature section.
4
Click + Create new, name your signature (e.g. Work 2026), click inside the signature text area, and press Ctrl + V (or Cmd + V on Mac) to paste.
5
Under Signature defaults, select your new signature for "For new emails use" and "On reply/forward use".
6
Scroll to the bottom of the page and click "Save Changes". You're all set! 🎉
