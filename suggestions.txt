PART 2: 📄 PDF TOOLS SECTION EXPANSION IDEAS
================================================================================

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. 📑 PDF EDITING, ANNOTATION & PAGE MANAGEMENT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

5.1. PDF Page Organizer, Reorder & Rotator (Drag & Drop)
     - Visual grid of PDF page thumbnails with smooth drag-and-drop reordering.
     - Rotate individual pages or all pages (90° clockwise, 90° counter-clockwise, 180°).
     - Delete unwanted pages or duplicate specific pages with 1 click.
     - Instant client-side re-bundling and PDF download using pdf-lib.

5.2. PDF Watermark & Page Numbering Tool
     - Text Watermark: add customizable text ("CONFIDENTIAL", "DRAFT", "COPY") with opacity, rotation angle, font size, and color controls.
     - Image Watermark: stamp transparent PNG logos, company badges, or seals onto pages.
     - Page Numbering: add headers/footers with dynamic formats ("Page X of Y", "X", "- X -"), position selectors (Top/Bottom, Left/Center/Right), and page range exclusions (e.g. skip cover page).

5.3. PDF Signature, Stamp & Annotation Studio
     - Draw a digital signature on canvas, type signature with cursive fonts, or upload a signature image.
     - Position, scale, and place signatures, date stamps, and "APPROVED" / "PAID" / "CONFIDENTIAL" badges on any page.
     - Add text notes, highlight boxes, and rectangular markup.
     - Flatten and export signed documents client-side without quality loss.

5.4. PDF Redactor & Sanitizer (Permanent Text/Area Blackout)
     - Securely redact sensitive personal info, credit cards, SSNs, or proprietary data by drawing blackout rectangles over pages.
     - True client-side flattening/rasterization of redacted sections to guarantee text underneath cannot be copied or recovered.
     - Built-in keyword search & redact helper.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6. ⚙️ PDF CONVERSION, EXTRACTION & OPTIMIZATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

6.1. PDF OCR & Searchable Text Extractor
     - Extract selectable digital text from PDF documents.
     - Scanned Document OCR: run 100% in-browser OCR (via Tesseract.js) to recognize text from scanned paper PDFs or photos without uploading files to any external server.
     - Export recognized text as TXT, Markdown, or Searchable PDF.

6.2. PDF Grayscale & Print Ink Saver
     - Convert color PDFs into clean monochrome or high-contrast grayscale to save printer ink and toner.
     - Brightness, contrast, and threshold adjustment sliders for enhancing faint scanned receipts or documents.

6.3. PDF N-Up & Booklet Layout Creator (2-Up, 4-Up, Handouts)
     - Combine multiple PDF pages into a single sheet (2 pages per sheet, 4 pages per sheet, or presentation handout style).
     - Booklet imposition mode: rearranges pages into printable double-sided folded booklet sequence.

6.4. PDF Metadata & Document Properties Editor
     - View and modify document metadata: Title, Author, Subject, Keywords, Creator, and Creation Date.
     - 1-click "Sanitize & Strip All Metadata" for privacy and anonymization before publishing documents publicly.

6.5. PDF Form Filler & Data Exporter
     - View and fill interactive AcroForm fields (text fields, checkboxes, radio buttons, dropdowns) directly in the browser.
     - Export completed PDF or extract all form field data into JSON/CSV spreadsheets.
