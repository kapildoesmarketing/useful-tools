Below is a curated list of creative, interactive, and high-engagement tool
ideas tailored for the "Fun Tools" category on go/usefultools. All ideas are
designed to be 100% client-side (no backend required), instant-loading, and
optimized for Google Sites embedding.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. 🎵 INTERACTIVE MUSIC & AUDIO TOYS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1.1. Virtual Piano & Polyphonic Synth Keyboard
     - Interactive 25/49/88-key keyboard playable via mouse, touch, or computer keys.
     - Multiple instrument presets: Grand Piano, Electric Rhodes, 80s Synth, Organ, Marimba.
     - Waveform oscillator selector (Sine, Square, Sawtooth, Triangle) with ADSR envelope.
     - Built-in Chord Assistant (1-click trigger for Major, Minor, 7th chords) and MIDI support.

1.2. 8-Bit Retro Chiptune & Game SFX Generator
     - Generate classic arcade & NES sound effects (Jump, Laser, Explosion, Coin, Power-up, Hit).
     - Procedural randomized sound generation with fine-tuning knobs (Pitch, Decay, Vibrato, Noise).
     - 1-click export to .WAV audio files for game developers and video creators.

1.3. Ambient Soundscape & Binaural Focus Generator
     - Calming layered sound mixer: Rain on Window, Thunder, Campfire, Coffee Shop, Ocean Waves, White Noise.
     - Binaural beat frequency generator (Alpha for focus, Theta for meditation, Delta for sleep).
     - Individual volume sliders and custom mix preset saving.

1.4. Xylophone & Steel Drum Studio
     - Colorful, tactile musical instrument with cheerful resonant acoustics.
     - Record & playback melodies with interactive visual note trails.
